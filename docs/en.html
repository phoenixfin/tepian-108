Tepian 108

Are you a Suikoden fan? What if the 108 Stars gathered in Indonesia, in 2030? We are writing the story and building the game, openly, together.

What it is

Tepian 108 is an open, collaborative universe: a novel, short stories, regional research, art, music, and an indie RPG. It is inspired by the 108-hero structure of Water Margin and by games like Suikoden, Exit Fate, and Eiyuden Chronicle: recruiting people one by one, watching a home base grow because of them, and living with the cost of every choice.

The setting is Indonesia in 2030. After a disputed 2029 election, the country is run by four conglomerate families and a quiet coordinating agency. A young man from Kalimantan, freshly released after being wrongfully arrested, opens a communal kitchen in an abandoned textile factory in Jakarta. It grows into a movement of mutual care, hunted, split, and spread across the archipelago. 108 people on one blacklist finally find each other on 17 August 2030.

Everything in the story is fiction. Places are real; people, parties, companies, and institutions are invented.

What exists so far

  • A story bible: world, characters, five-act plot
  • 108 character frames: 23 named core characters, 85 with names deliberately left blank
  • 17 regional nodes, from Medan to Merauke
  • Six segments, each rooted in a different region
  • A game design document (Godot 4) and a gray-box prototype scaffold

How to take part

Only the skeleton is locked: the main plot, the map of major nodes, and the core characters. Names, backgrounds, voices, scenes, and many stories are open. Several people can work on the same character as long as they coordinate.

Write

Name a character, write their voice and scenes, or describe a place you know.

Illustrate and design

Portraits, landscapes, sprites, UI, logos, maps.

Music and sound

Soundtrack arrangements, BGM, field recordings, voice acting.

Game

System design, Godot code, level design, playtesting.
